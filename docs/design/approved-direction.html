What changed, and what didn't

Kept from Catalogue

Six categories as the organising spine, a real description on every tool, and a quiet ground that lets the content carry the page. No motif to maintain across 15 tools — just a panel, a type scale and a colour code.

Typography

System sans throughout — SF on your machines — at tight tracking with a real weight range. Mono is reserved for data only: records, paths, counts. No serif anywhere.

Panels, not rows

Cards with a hairline border, a faint top highlight and a category-tinted glow on hover. Density comes from Grafana; the restraint of the surface comes from Linear.

Colour is still a code

Six hues drive the icon tile, the group rule, the hover glow, the primary button and the record type column. It all derives from category in the manifest, so a new tool colours itself.

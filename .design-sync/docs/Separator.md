A hairline rule on the outline token, for separating groups inside one surface.

Use sparingly: most separation in this system comes from the panel border and from spacing. Reach for a Separator only when two groups genuinely share one surface and need a boundary.

**Props:** `orientation` (`"horizontal"` default, or `"vertical"`), `decorative`.

```jsx
<Separator />
<Separator orientation="vertical" className="h-6" />
```
